Star Port — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Star Port is a single-player virtual reality game for Meta Quest, made by North Star Software.

What we collect

Only leaderboard scores. Star Port does not sell or share personal information, and has no ads, no analytics and no accounts of its own.

  • Leaderboards (Meta Horizon platform): when you finish a race, or dock with a new best net worth or bounty count, the game posts that score to Meta's leaderboard service, using your Meta Horizon profile. Other players see your Meta display name next to your score. A race entry also carries a short recording of your ship's path round the course (positions and headings only), so other players can race it as a "ghost". Nothing else is sent. This is handled by Meta under Meta's privacy policy; Star Port has no server of its own and keeps no copy.
  • The game checks with the Meta platform that it was obtained from the store (an entitlement check).
  • The Meta platform software it is built on (the Meta XR SDK) may send standard technical telemetry to Meta, covered by Meta's own privacy policy.
  • Hand tracking and head tracking are used only on your headset, in real time, to let you play. That data is handled by the Meta Quest system; Star Port never records or sends it anywhere.

What stays on your headset

Your saved game (credits, cargo, ship, progress), your best race recordings and your settings (comfort vignette, turn speed, music) are stored only on your headset, in the app's own private storage. Uninstalling Star Port deletes them.

Children

Star Port does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes again (for example, if online trading between players is added), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the new policy will say exactly what is collected and why.
