Star Port — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Star Port is a single-player virtual reality game for Meta Quest, made by North Star Software.

What we collect

Nothing. Star Port does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information.

  • The game has no accounts, no online features, no analytics and no advertising.
  • Star Port itself sends nothing over the internet. The Meta platform software it is built on (the Meta XR SDK) may send standard technical telemetry to Meta, covered by Meta's own privacy policy.
  • Hand tracking and head tracking are used only on your headset, in real time, to let you play. That data is handled by the Meta Quest system; Star Port never records or sends it anywhere.

What stays on your headset

Your saved game (credits, cargo, ship, progress) and your settings (comfort vignette, turn speed, music) are stored only on your headset, in the app's own private storage. Uninstalling Star Port deletes them.

Children

Star Port does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if online trading between players is added), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the new policy will say exactly what is collected and why.
